# HealthConnect Rwanda | Week 8 Task 7

**Data Analytics · Key Business Insights**

HealthConnect can use the dashboard to review recorded workload, overall collections and patient feedback. Decisions about missed appointments, facility performance or bed capacity require more complete records.

| Priority insight | What my analysis found | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Booking volumes provide a workload baseline.** | The extract contains **5,000 bookings**, with **400–440** recorded per month. There is no field showing whether a patient attended, missed, cancelled or rescheduled. | The counts help describe recorded booking volume. They do not show which patients or facilities have more no-shows, or whether reminders would help. |
| **2. Collections reconcile overall, but facility charts cover a small share.** | The exact collections card matched **RWF 8,389,454,556**. After the chart correction, the sampled facility and department values matched the source. Only **8.28%** of payment value links to a recorded booking; **RWF 7,694,903,236** remains in the blank group. | Management can review overall collections and the **RWF 919,947,405** billed-minus-collected balance. The named bars show only the linked share, and the balance does not establish how much is overdue or whether the clinic made a loss. |
| **3. Overall feedback is more informative than a facility ranking.** | Average satisfaction was **2.99 out of 5** across **21,064 responses**. **5,283 (25.1%)** were tagged Poor. Only **1,756 (8.34%)** of all responses linked to an appointment. | The overall result gives the clinic a reason to review patient support. Facility comparisons reflect a small linked subset, and the appointment month cannot establish when patients submitted feedback. |
| **4. Bed status describes a snapshot.** | All **2,000 beds** were marked Available in the supplied file. None were marked Occupied, so the dashboard showed **0.0%** for that snapshot. | The snapshot shows the recorded status of the beds. It cannot establish utilisation over time or justify reducing capacity. |
