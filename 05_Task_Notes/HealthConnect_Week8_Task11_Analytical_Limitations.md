# HealthConnect Rwanda | Week 8 Task 11

**Data Analytics · Analytical Limitations**

The dashboard supports a review of recorded bookings, overall collections and overall patient feedback. The limits below affect which comparisons can be made and how the figures should be presented.

| Limitation | What it means for the findings | How I report it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Attendance outcomes are missing.** The appointment extract contains **5,000 booking IDs** and has no outcome field. The Revenue Summary reports **60,809 appointments** in aggregate, without individual IDs. | Neither file establishes a no-show rate. The summary total cannot identify individual bookings or explain why an ID is missing from the extract. | Label the 5,000 as **Recorded Appointments**. Keep the summary count separate and report no-show rates only after attendance outcomes are available. |
| **Facility and department breakdowns have incomplete coverage.** Only **8.28% of payment value** and **8.34% of feedback responses** link to an appointment in the extract. | The named groups describe the linked subset. Even when a bar matches its source total, it does not represent every payment or feedback response for that group. | Keep unmatched payments in the overall total and visible as **(Blank)** or **Unattributed**. State the linked share and response counts beside group comparisons. |
| **Facility assignments conflict with department records.** In **4,744 appointment records**, the recorded facility differs from the facility associated with the recorded department in the master data. | A department-based join can assign an appointment to a different facility from the one recorded on the booking. The mismatch alone does not show which assignment is correct. | Preserve the original IDs. Reconcile assignments with the data owner before using facility and department comparisons to rank performance or allocate resources. |
| **Financial totals have different definitions.** Reported revenue comes from the monthly summary; collected payments come from the payment records. The available dates and expense records are insufficient for aging or profitability analysis. | **RWF 919,947,405** is the difference between billed and collected amounts. It does not establish overdue balances or a loss. Missing status dates also prevent confirming whether a billing status was wrong when it was recorded. | Label reported revenue, payments and the billed-minus-collected balance separately. Obtain due dates, payment dates and expense records for those further analyses. |
| **Feedback dates and bed history are unavailable.** Feedback has no submission date, and none of the **2,000 beds** was marked Occupied in the supplied snapshot. | A feedback chart by appointment month cannot show when patients submitted feedback. The **0.0%** recorded bed occupancy does not measure utilisation over time. | Describe feedback by **linked appointment month** and bed status as a **snapshot**. Collect dated records before presenting time trends. |
