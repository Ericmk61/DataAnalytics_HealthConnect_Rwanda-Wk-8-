# HealthConnect Rwanda | Week 8 Task 8

**Data Analytics · Actionable Recommendations**

**First priority: record what happens to each booking.** The current extract can show workload, but HealthConnect needs a reliable attendance baseline before testing reminders or changes to rescheduling. Work on collections, feedback and capacity reporting can proceed in parallel.

| Priority | Recommended action | Proposed lead | How to measure progress |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Capture appointment outcomes.** | Record the scheduled time, final status and outcome timestamp for each booking. Use consistent statuses for attended, missed, cancelled and rescheduled. Agree when a booking can be closed as missed and keep unknown outcomes visible. | Scheduling and clinic data owners | Review the share of eligible bookings with a known outcome each week. Agree on a coverage threshold before publishing a no-show rate. |
| **2. Improve collection attribution.** | Reconcile Billing and Payments against the authoritative appointment register. Keep amounts without an appointment link visible as **Unattributed** and verify facility and department assignments before comparing groups. | Finance and Data Analytics | Track the linked share of payment value against the current **8.28%**. Check that linked and unattributed amounts add to the overall collections total on each refresh. |
| **3. Make patient feedback easier to act on.** | Capture submission dates and validate appointment references. Use the overall results to guide a review of patient support, and show response counts beside facility averages. | Patient Experience and Data Analytics | Track linkage against the current **8.34%** and review date completeness and response counts by facility before making comparisons. |
| **4. Build a history of bed occupancy.** | Record occupied and staffed beds by ward at a consistent time each day, using a common definition across sites. Retain the existing 2,000-bed extract as a status snapshot. | Ward operations | Monitor the share of ward-days with complete records. Once coverage is adequate, report occupied bed-days as a share of staffed bed-days. |
| **5. Pilot a scheduling change after the baseline is reliable.** | Compare reminders or an easier rescheduling process with usual booking support for comparable eligible appointments. If the pilot includes an AI assistant, limit it to routine scheduling questions using approved clinic information and route unresolved requests to staff. | Clinic Operations and Data Analytics | Compare no-show rates, completed reschedules and patient feedback between pilot and comparison groups. Report outcome coverage for both before deciding whether to expand. |
